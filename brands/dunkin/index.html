Zelta Design, Nutley, New Jersey

Zelta Design's portfolio includes 26 Dunkin' locations across 2 states. Repeat work for one brand changes what the drawings start from: the current prototype, the equipment schedule, the finish standards and the approvals the brand requires before a landlord or a building department sees anything. Each location still gets its own survey, its own code path and its own plan-review strategy, because a Dunkin' in a Manhattan ground floor and a freestanding one with a drive-thru answer to different chapters of the code. What carries over is the part that usually costs time: knowing which comments a reviewer will raise, and answering them in the first submission rather than the third.

Programs: Dunkin NextGen (6), Dunkin OMNI (6). Formats: remodel (7), drive-thru (6), multi-brand combo (6), walk-up window (4).
