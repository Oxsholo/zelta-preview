03 Common questions

What Operators Ask Us

How long does permitting take?

Timing depends on the project and the local building department. We review the approval requirements, track submissions and respond to plan-review comments.

Do you manage permit applications?

Yes. We handle applications, submissions, review tracking, comment responses and resubmittals. Depending on the project, approvals may include building, fire, health-department and signage permits.

Can you work on locations in different states?

We are licensed in 24 states and Washington, D.C., with one team coordinating your brand standards, drawings and local site requirements.

Do you know our brand's design standards?

We have worked with 40+ brands, including Dunkin', Taco Bell, Popeyes and Dave's Hot Chicken. Tell us your brand and format so we can discuss relevant projects.

How do you coordinate multiple projects?

Our team tracks project schedules, permit submissions and drawing revisions in a shared workspace. We keep brand references and project documents together so each location uses the correct requirements.

Can your team visit sites outside New Jersey?

Yes. Our own aircraft supports site surveys and coordination visits across the states where we are licensed, and beyond.

What do you need to get started?

Your lease or letter of intent, available landlord drawings and brand approval package. We then review the site survey and code requirements.

What if my project is in a state you're not licensed in?

Tell us about the project. We add licenses as our clients grow into new states, and we can typically be licensed in time for your permit submission.
