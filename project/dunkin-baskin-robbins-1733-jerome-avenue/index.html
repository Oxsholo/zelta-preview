Dunkin'

Bronx
1733 Jerome Avenue, Bronx, NY 10453

This Mount Eden location combines Dunkin' and Baskin-Robbins with a drive-thru, on a corner site built for both car and walk-in traffic. Inside, a curved donut case sits near the entry with the order counter and a self-service kiosk set back near the dining area, so mobile pickup and in-person ordering don't collide. Cream tile, wood-look counters, and a Baskin-Robbins freezer wall carry both brands' colors without clashing. The layout lets drive-thru, counter service, and seated guests all move through without crossing paths.
