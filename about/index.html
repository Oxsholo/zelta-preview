Est. About Zelta Design

Restaurant & Commercial Architects

The Practice Nutley, New Jersey

Zelta Design is a full-service architectural firm specializing in restaurants. We work with national brands, franchise groups and independent operators on coffee shops, quick-service restaurants and sit-down dining. Our architects and engineers have completed more than 1,000 restaurant projects.

The majority of our clients operate 20+ locations. Licensed in 25 states, we handle design, construction documents, permitting and coordination through construction. Each project is planned around the client's site, requirements and budget.
