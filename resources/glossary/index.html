Restaurant Design & Permitting Glossary

The terms you will meet in drawings, leases and permit conversations, in plain English. These are general meanings; the applicable code, local authority and project agreements determine the requirements for your site.

A-2 occupancy

A building-code category for assembly uses involving food or drink, including many restaurants. Some small dining spaces fall into another category under the applicable code.

Accessibility

Design that allows people with disabilities to enter, move through and use a space and its services.

Restaurant ADA Requirements: Entrances, Seating and Restrooms
ADA

The Americans with Disabilities Act, a federal civil rights law that includes accessibility obligations for businesses open to the public.

Restaurant ADA Requirements: Entrances, Seating and Restrooms
AHJ

Authority having jurisdiction: the agency or official responsible for interpreting and enforcing a particular code or approval requirement.

Restaurant Architect, Engineer, Expediter or Contractor?
As-built drawings

Drawings recording how work was installed, including changes from the original design. Their completeness and accuracy depend on how they were prepared.

As-Built Drawings and Site Surveys for Restaurants
Brand prototype

A standard design package a franchise brand uses as a starting point for its locations. It must be adapted to the actual site.

How Franchise Brand Design Approvals Work
Building permit

Approval from the responsible authority to carry out a defined scope of building work, subject to the permit conditions and required inspections.

Restaurant Build-Out Timeline: From Lease to Opening
Certificate of occupancy

An official document establishing the approved use or occupancy of a building or space. The process for obtaining or changing it depends on the jurisdiction.

How Do You Get a Certificate of Occupancy for a Restaurant?
Change of occupancy

A change in a building or space that affects its occupancy classification or applicable code requirements. It may require review even when little physical work is planned.

Turning a Retail Space into a Restaurant: Change of Use
Change of use

A change in the activity carried on in a property, such as converting a shop into a restaurant. Zoning and building authorities may each need to review it.

Cold dark shell

An unfinished space delivered with limited building services and interior finishes. The lease and work letter establish exactly what the landlord provides.

Landlord Work vs Tenant Work: Shell Types and the Work Letter
Construction administration

Design-team services during construction, such as reviewing submittals, answering questions and observing work. The agreement defines the services included.

What Your Restaurant Architect Does During Construction
Construction documents

The coordinated drawings and specifications that describe the work to be priced, permitted and built.

Restaurant Permit Drawings: What's in the Set
Deferred submittal

A portion of the design submitted for approval after the initial permit application, with the reviewing authority’s permission.

Dunnage

In rooftop equipment work, a supporting framework that carries equipment loads to the building structure.

Egress

The route people use to leave a building, including the path through the space, exits and the discharge to a safe outdoor area.

Equipment schedule

A list identifying equipment and the information needed to coordinate it, such as dimensions, power, water, gas and drainage connections.

Existing conditions survey

An investigation documenting the space and visible building systems before design begins. Concealed conditions may need further investigation.

Expediter

A person or firm that helps coordinate permit filings, agency communication and application tracking. An expediter does not replace the authority that reviews and approves the work.

Grease interceptor

A plumbing device that separates fats, oils and grease from wastewater before it reaches the sewer. Selection and sizing depend on the fixtures served and local requirements.

Health department plan review

Review of a proposed food establishment’s layout, equipment and operations for food-safety requirements before construction or opening.

Landlord work letter
